# Project Global Rules (USStockVCP)

## Language & Communication Rule (語言與回覆規範)

- **Always respond in Traditional Chinese (繁體中文)**: Regardless of whether the user asks a question in English or Chinese, you MUST always provide all explanations, analyses, reports, and answers in **Traditional Chinese (繁體中文)** unless the user explicitly instructs otherwise for a specific turn.
- Technical terms, stock tickers (e.g., `NVDA`, `PLTR`, `APP`), strategy codes (e.g., `M1`–`M4`, `D2b`, `D3b`), and code identifiers may retain their original English names alongside Traditional Chinese explanations for clarity.
